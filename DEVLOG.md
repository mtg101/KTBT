# KTBT: Devlog

A running record of what we've done on KTBT and why, newest first. What's coming next is in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md), and AI quirks are in [LLM-TUNING.md](LLM-TUNING.md).

## 14 November 2026

- **Several at once stage done:** the tent now has **five stools**, and customers overlap.
  - **A wider tent:** the grey-box tent now stretches nearly the full width of the screen, with the stove at the left end. Dishes are small unlabelled shapes (a bottle for a drink, a bowl for food, three little dishes for banchan), with names on hover; drinks and banchan sit to a customer's left, food to their right. The info panel lists everyone at the counter by stool, what's on their table, what's cooking and their bill so far, plus how many are waiting for a stool.
  - **One cook:** drinks, odeng (from the simmering pot) and plain rice come straight away; cooked dishes (each with a cooking time in `menu.gd`, from 4 game minutes for sundae to 12 for dakgalbi) queue at the stove, one at a time, and the owner carries each over when it's ready. The AI is told what's still cooking, so customers can mention waiting for it but never eat it early.
  - **The owner moves round:** one conversation at a time, choosing by priority: greet someone new (their order and a first exchange), carry over food that's ready, settle up with someone who's finished, otherwise chat for an exchange or two with whoever's been left alone longest. It never switches between the owner's line and the customer's reply. The transcript says when the owner turns back to someone, and so does that customer's AI context; with two or more in, the owner's lines say who they're talking to.
  - **Full tent:** people who can't get a stool wait; after 30 game minutes they give up, and a passer-by walks along the street, stops to look in at the full stools, and carries on.
  - **3am:** last orders. Every conversation wraps up within a line or two, nobody new sits down, and the clock runs on past 3am until the last customer has gone.
  - **Changes:** **Next customer** now stays clickable (each click adds someone to the queue for a stool), and **Close the bar** works mid-visit: the owner finishes their current line first. Later orders still happen on the customer's own turns.
  - **Known limit:** customers stay until their conversation has run its course, so on a busy night the stools stay full for hours. Fine while only the owner talks; a **How long people stay** idea in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) fixes it before customers can chat to each other.
  - **Tests:** 14 new (cooking times, what's served straight away, the "still cooking" table text, last orders, and click-through tests for several customers at once, five stools, giving up and walking past, the stove, the AI hearing what's cooking, and the owner moving round), **207** in total, all passing.
- **A full night stage done:** each time a bar opens, it runs a whole night, from **6pm to 3am**.
  - **The clock:** always runs, in real time, whatever's happening, so a night takes about **5 minutes** (about **1 minute** on Fast). The top of the screen shows the night number, day, time and weather. The first version only moved the clock between customers, adding fixed game time per visit, but that wouldn't work once several customers overlap, so the clock now runs regardless.
  - **Arrivals:** customers turn up on their own, at times that suit the hour: a few at opening, busiest from 9pm to midnight, thinning out after 1am, nobody new after 2:30am. Each arrival is timed from the last one's arrival, so a long conversation doesn't hold the night up.
  - **One stool for now:** a customer who's due while the stool is taken waits; after 30 game minutes they give up and walk on (the transcript says so). So until the Several at once stage, how many customers a night gets depends on how fast the AI is.
  - **Next customer** still works mid-night, for testing (and later it might be you walking in).
  - **Days and weather:** each new bar starts on a random day of the week and goes round the week night by night; each night picks its weather at 6pm.
  - **3am:** once the last customer finishes, the owner closes up and a **night summary** appears: customers, the night's takings, the total, and the night's most memorable line (for now, the longest thing a customer said). **Open for another night**, or **Close the bar**.
  - **Saves:** each bar now counts its nights and keeps a log of recent ones; the title screen shows nights as well as visits. A night you leave before 3am isn't counted, though each visit's takings are saved as before.
  - **Tests:** 23 new (the clock, arrivals, days of the week and memorable lines; saving nights; and 9 click-through tests for a whole night), **193** in total, all passing. The older click-through tests hold the clock still (`Visit.run_clock = false`) so automatic customers don't interfere.
- **Bars and saves stage done:** the game now opens on a **title screen**: **New bar**, or a list of saved bars (name and meaning, owner, visits, takings, last played; newest first).
  - **New bars:** the AI invents the owner, then names the tent with a short **Korean** name in English letters plus its meaning ("Byeolbit Pocha", Starlight Tent). If the AI can't, the game names it after the owner ("Mi-sook-ne Pocha"). Offline mode has its own names.
  - **Saves:** one readable JSON file per bar in `saves/` in the user data folder, written safely (temporary file first, then swapped in; a crash leaves a `.tmp` that loading recovers). Each holds the owner, name, takings, visit count and a short history of recent visits.
  - **Autosave:** after creating a bar, after every visit, on **Close the bar** (back to the title) and when the window closes. A visit still going when you quit just ends; resuming mid-conversation is a later idea.
  - **Under the hood:** a `Game` autoload keeps track of the open bar; `save_store.gd` reads and writes saves.
  - **Tests:** 36 new (save files, bar names, and 12 click-through tests for the title screen, new and saved bars, and autosave), **170** in total, all passing.
  - **Gotchas learned:** editing `project.godot` by hand (to add the autoload) needs **Project → Reload Current Project** before Godot sees it; and click-through tests must let container layouts settle for a frame or two before aiming a click.
- **Tightened a retry test:** the bad-owner test now proves the bad reply was used, rejected and replaced, not just that some owner appeared.
- **GdUnit4's run file ignored:** `addons/gdUnit4/GdUnitRunner.cfg` changes on every test run, so it's now in `.gitignore` and untracked.

## 13 November 2026

- **More room for the dialogue:** the game now runs at 1920×1080 in a normal window (was 1280×720). The grey-box tent stays the same size, centred at the top, and the transcript panel gets the rest of the screen, roughly three to four times as many lines, in slightly bigger text. The layout comes from three constants at the top of `visit.gd` (`SCREEN`, `PANELS_TOP`, `INFO_WIDTH`).
- **Later orders fixed:** later orders sometimes asked for more than they got (e.g. "another tteokbokki" when only a drink was ordered), because the line check let anything already on the table through and didn't notice aliases like "rice". Now everything asked for must be in the order, a dish on the table can only be mentioned as "this/the …", and order lines catch aliases ("fried rice" counted once). 6 new tests, **134** in total, all passing. Details in [LLM-TUNING.md](LLM-TUNING.md).
- **Tests stage done:** click-through tests now load the real visit scene, click **Next customer** with a simulated mouse, and check whole visits play out on the offline AI: exact takings, the customer leaving, retries, the soju-and-odeng fallback, Try again, and Ollama being unreachable. To support them, key UI nodes got fixed names and the scene got test hooks for pause length and a fixed random seed. Writing the "Ollama unreachable" test exposed a real problem: with nothing listening, Windows could take minutes to give up, so `auto` mode now asks Ollama "are you there?" with a 3-second limit at startup and goes offline straight away if not. **128 tests**, all passing.
- **AI settings and offline mode:** each machine now has an `ai_settings.cfg` (in Godot's user data folder) choosing the AI backend (`auto`, `ollama` or `canned`), the Ollama server and the model, so the desktop and laptop can share the project. Offline mode (`canned_ai.gd`) answers instantly from pre-written owners, customers, orders and lines, built to pass the game's rules, and shows an **OFFLINE MODE** badge; in `auto` the game switches to it by itself if Ollama fails. It's also the fake AI for the coming click-through tests. Stage numbers were tidied out of the code and screen titles. 12 new tests, 118 in total, all passing. Setup documented in `TOOLCHAIN.md` section 5b.
- **Tuning notes renamed:** `TUNING.md` is now `LLM-TUNING.md` (title "LLM Tuning notes"), to make clear it's about tuning the local LLM's output. Links and test comments updated.
- **Ordering like a real pojangmacha:** a run of playtesting fixes, each with regression tests (details in [LLM-TUNING.md](LLM-TUNING.md)):
  - **Food stays on the table:** the style guide no longer names any dish, and conversation lines that mention food that isn't on the table are rejected.
  - **Sensible orders:** a drink and a food every time, usually one main and one drink, with the game deciding who's hungry (two mains) or having a rough night (two drinks). Menu foods are now marked main or side, and free banchan comes with every order.
  - **Later orders:** half of customers order once, 40% order more partway through, 10% a third time: one more bottle, a side, or rice. New menu items: gonggibap (rice) and bokkeumbap (fried rice, only after a main). Later orders sound like ordering more, never come straight after the food, and add to the conversation rather than replacing it.
  - **Fast mode on by default** while we're testing a lot.
  - **Tests:** 106 in total, all passing.
- **Conversations that listen (a bit) better:** a visit produced a conversation where the owner and customer ignored each other (she ordered tteokbokki; he asked if she'd eat). Two quick prompt fixes: the serving now appears in the transcript the AI reads, and every dialogue prompt ends with a "what just happened" recap. Noticeably better, still needs work later; details in [LLM-TUNING.md](LLM-TUNING.md). 3 new regression tests, 64 in total, all passing.
- **First tests pass:** moved the game's rules (every check on the AI's replies, plus the bill, order names and text tidying) out of `visit.gd` into a new `scripts/rules.gd`, so they can be tested without a scene or the AI. Wrote 61 GdUnit4 tests in `tests/`: menu (12), rules (40) and prompts (9), including regression tests for each fixed issue in [LLM-TUNING.md](LLM-TUNING.md). All 61 pass first time, which also confirms GdUnit4 v6.2.0 works with Godot 4.7.2.
- **GdUnit4 installed:** the project has moved to `godot-gdunit-labs/gdUnit4` on GitHub, where the latest release is v6.2.2 (7 October 2026), officially supporting Godot up to 4.7.1. Godot 4.7 has replaced the old AssetLib tab with the new Godot Asset Store, which only had v6.2.0, so we installed that (same v6.2 line) and enabled the plugin. The GdUnit tab appears in the editor. Install steps and version table updated in `TOOLCHAIN.md`.
- **Future ideas mapped out:** added to [PLAN.md](PLAN.md)'s later ideas, grouped as essentials, richer play, presentation and lower priority. New: bar-specific menus (catalogue staples plus AI signature dishes), character art (silhouettes, then modular portraits; live AI portraits noted as theoretically possible), ambience, voices (babble, system voices or AI voices), content boundaries as a setting, prepare-ahead AI calls, settings and accessibility, packaging and first-run setup, a calendar with Korean holidays, drama arcs, a journal and trope book, and more.
- **Docs restructured:** the plan no longer numbers its stages (we'd already had to slot in a "stage 2.5"), and only lists what's still to do. Finished work now lives in this devlog, and tuning notes moved to their own file (now [LLM-TUNING.md](LLM-TUNING.md)).
- **No-GPU plan:** started working on a laptop without an NVIDIA GPU, so the local LLM can't run well there. Planned AI settings (backend, server address, model) and an offline **canned AI** mode as part of the Tests stage, and documented three ways to work without a suitable GPU in [TOOLCHAIN.md](TOOLCHAIN.md#5a-machines-without-a-suitable-gpu): canned AI, a small model on the CPU, or the desktop's Ollama over the home network.
- **Laptop setup:** cloned the repo to the laptop. Claude's shell isn't available there (the desktop app doesn't offer it on that machine, though virtualization, memory and disk all check out), so on the laptop Claude edits files by copying them back and forth, and the developer runs Godot, tests and git.
- **Testing planned:** chose **GdUnit4** over GUT, for click-through UI tests, built-in mocks, and VS Code and GitHub integration. Wrote [TESTS.md](TESTS.md) and a GdUnit4 section in `TOOLCHAIN.md`. Note: GdUnit4 v6.2.x lists support up to Godot 4.7.1, and we're on 4.7.2, so we'll confirm it works when installing.

## 12 November 2026

- **Concept and setup:** settled on Godot 4 (free, open source, scripts that Claude can write as plain text) and a local LLM via Ollama, to keep the game free to run. Wrote [CONCEPT.md](CONCEPT.md) and [TOOLCHAIN.md](TOOLCHAIN.md). Toolchain verified on the desktop: Godot 4.7.2, Ollama with `llama3.1:8b`, RTX 3080 12GB. The only surprise was a slow first reply, which turned out to be the model loading into GPU memory.

- **Pipeline test stage done:** press a button, and the local LLM invents a customer whose profile appears on screen. Godot, Ollama and the model talked to each other first time.
- **One visit stage done:** the AI invents an owner, then one customer at a time arrives, orders from the menu, is served automatically, chats with the owner, pays and leaves, all in grey-box art. The owner was pulled forward from the Bars and saves stage so customers have someone to talk to; it isn't saved yet. The game checks every AI reply and retries with feedback when it's wrong.
- **Tuning fixes:** fixed customers ordering twice with mismatched orders, and owners with wrong or speaker-dependent titles; added an interim fix for strangers acting like regulars. Details in [LLM-TUNING.md](LLM-TUNING.md).
- **Small touches:** customers now arrive from, and leave towards, a random side of the street.
- **Docs:** split the concept from the plan, and standardised list formatting.
