# KTBT: LLM Tuning notes

Quirks we've noticed in the AI's output, why they happen, and how we handle them. Open issues are waiting for a fix; fixed ones stay here as a record of what works with a small local model, and each should get a test (see [TESTS.md](TESTS.md#bugs-become-tests)).

**Lesson so far:** when a small model keeps doing something odd, changing what it's asked to produce, or having the game decide and check the fact itself, works better than adding more instructions to the prompt.

## Open

- **The diary repeating the prompt (fixed 15 Nov 2026):** the first diary entry began "Wrote tonight's entry in the diary", echoing the prompt ("is writing tonight's entry in the diary they keep"). The prompt now just gives the night and says to start straight in, as people do in a diary; the game rejects an entry that opens by talking about writing it.
- **Asking for phrases without naming them (15 Nov 2026, working):** to get Chef-my-Fridge food lines ("it must be good", "you eat well") without the model saying the same words every time, the game gives the situation, not the phrase: a line of food culture in the style guide, and now and then a direction like "They're eating like they haven't eaten all day." It found its own words ("You don't look like someone who eats much, but you have a good appetite."). The same trick should work for other tropes.
- **TV reactions (tuned 15 Nov 2026, to watch):** after a spell of talk, Llama 3.1 8B rates the biggest moment from 1 to 5 and writes a caption. Asked just "was it a big moment?", it said yes about 16 times in 20. With only a 4 or 5 getting a caption, 7 showed in six nights, but 40 checks had been skipped while the owner was busy (and the prompt itself said a 3 was "none"). At 3 (with the prompt's wording now from the same setting as the check, `Captions.REACTION_MIN_SIZE`), every check said yes and the captions went soft and summary-like ("BOSS NEEDS A BREAK", "LOOKING OUT FOR YOU"), mostly "warm". So it's back to 4, with put-off checks now waiting for the owner instead of being lost. To watch: a few a night, and teasing rather than summing up.
- **The owner's reason for closing early (new 15 Nov 2026, to watch):** when the player closes up early, the AI gives the owner's reason, said to the counter, and a TV caption for it (two to five words, in capitals, no names but the owner's). The prompt suggests a few kinds (tired bones, family, a bad feeling, the weather, an early start) without giving lines; the examples in it are captions only. To watch: whether it always reaches for the weather or the knees, and whether captions copy the two examples.
- **Real people and brands (rule added 15 Nov 2026, to watch):** Llama likes dropping in real names: games ("League of Legends" turned up in a chat), and it would happily name a famous TV chef or a coffee brand. The style guide says never to, and a list of obvious brands is checked (`Talk.REAL_BRANDS`); real people can't be listed reliably, so watch transcripts for celebrities, shows and games by name, and add any brand that slips through to the list.
- **Short made-up Korean (partly fixed 15 Nov 2026):** two-word bits of invented romanised Korean got past the made-up-Korean check, which needs three ("Mandeulso, go na?", "Banchan na, makgeolli haetjuseyo?"). Now one word with a Korean verb ending ("-seyo", "-nida", "-haeyo"…), which no English word has, is enough, and every word in the glossary (now with annyeonghaseyo, gomawoyo, mianhae, gwaenchana and more) counts as real. Still missed: a lone made-up word with no telltale ending ("Mandeulso").
- **Last night's weather (prompt changed 15 Nov 2026, to check):** on a drizzly night the owner said "Fog's coming in", and the diary had fog too: the night before was foggy, and the regulars' memories and the recap carry it. Every prompt's moment now says it's "tonight's only weather, whatever it was before".
- **Copying the example teaser (prompt changed 15 Nov 2026, to check):** the recap's teaser began "Will they come back, now that…", the prompt's own example. The game now picks one of eight examples for each recap (`Story.RECAP_TEASER_EXAMPLES`, with no names in them), and asks for one in that spirit but its own.
- **Samey openings (15 Nov 2026, to watch):** left to itself, Llama 3.1 8B opened 67 of 115 orders "Ajumma, I'll have a…" or "Ajumma, just a…", and the owner's first word with a customer was "You look like you've been…" 18 times. The same approach as the food lines: no phrases, but now and then a situation (how they order tonight, how the owner opens), each only when it's true. The stats show the commonest start, to see whether it comes down. The first night with it: 8 of 20 orders (was 67 of 115), and the owner's openers all differed.
- **The first thing on the menu gets ordered (15 Nov 2026, to watch):** about 60% of customers had tteokbokki, the first food on the menu the model saw (and the cheapest proper main), and nobody had dakgalbi for two nights. The menu's shuffled for each order now, with weather specials marked and cravings now and then; the stats' Orders line shows the spread.
- **Hints taken up (15 Nov 2026, to watch):** the cat's moments were taken up 0 of 4 one night: given only as a situation, the AI lets it go by. A cat moment now turns the conversation's topic to the cat too, which worked before (people talked about the cat when it was the topic); if it does, the food hints could go the same way. Table customs (pouring for each other, somaek mixed at the table, ssam with dakgalbi) and sharing moments are given as situations; the stats count how many lines seemed to take them up (a rough check for tell-tale words). Early on: somaek's every time, the rest hardly at all.
- **Terms of address in every line (prompt changed 15 Nov 2026, to check):** once each conversation was told which terms the two may use, Llama 3.1 8B used one in nearly every line ("Thanks, Imo", "Sure, Haksaeng"). All were allowed, just too many. The note now says most lines need none at all. If it's still heavy, only give the note now and then.
- **Customer chat share (tuned 15 Nov 2026):** varied from 4% to 29% of a night's lines. The "chat chances" stat showed it wasn't the AI or the dice: chats happened whenever they could, but the owner's voice was rarely free. Shorter owner spells when busy and higher chances brought it to about 18%. Not an AI quirk, in the end: one voice for the whole tent is the limit.
- **Prices, freebies and promises (open, 14 Nov 2026):** the owner names prices ("two thousand eight hundred won", when the bill was ₩26,000), gives things away ("last one free, kid"), and customers ask for more in ordinary lines without a later order. Parked in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).
- **Order rules (open, 14 Nov 2026):** the most common rejected reply in the night logs (5–12 a night, out of about 300 AI calls): an order that breaks one of the ordering rules (one drink, a food, one main unless hungry...). Each costs another call. The night logs now record the actual reasons (`rejection_reasons`); next step is to see which rule small Llama trips over and tune the order prompt.
- **Not listening to each other (partly fixed 13 Nov 2026, needs more work):** the owner and customer reply as if they hadn't heard each other. Example: Kim Ji-hyun orders "a cold beer and a plate of tteokbokki", both are served, and the owner replies "One beer, okay. But you eat, no?", then she answers "I'm good, thanks". Likely causes: (1) the transcript the AI sees holds only dialogue, so the serving never appears in it, and the owner's first line reads like a reply to the order rather than coming after the food arrives; (2) the line to respond to is buried in the middle of a long prompt, and small models pay most attention to the end; (3) the style guide's strong tropes ("scolds customers for not eating properly") pull harder than the facts. Done so far: the serving is now recorded in the transcript as a stage direction ("(Kim Min-seok serves: Beer, Tteokbokki.)"), and every dialogue prompt ends with a **WHAT JUST HAPPENED** recap of the last line said and anything after it, telling the AI to reply directly and stay consistent (`what_just_happened()` in `scripts/prompts.gd`). Conversations are noticeably better, but still not great. Still to try: toning down how hard the style guide pushes tropes, and comparing models (Llama 3.1 8B, Gemma, Qwen) with AI quality runs.
- **Name order:** the AI sometimes writes names Western-style (e.g. "Ji-hoon Kim" instead of "Kim Ji-hoon"), despite being asked for family name first. Minor. Fix later with a prompt example, or by having the AI give family and given names as separate fields that the game joins in the right order. Related idea: non-Korean visitors, in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).
- **"You've been here before" (partly fixed 15 Nov 2026, by regulars):** regulars now come back with the game's facts (visits, names known, their usual), so the owner can treat them as known; first-timers still get the "first visit" text. Still to watch: the owner treating a first-timer as a regular anyway. Original note: the owner sometimes treats a first-time customer as a regular ("you've been here before, haven't you?"), ignoring the first-visit note. Same root cause as the entry below: a trope (the owner who knows everyone) beating a fact. To be fixed properly when the game tracks who knows whom.
- **Strangers acting like regulars (interim fix 12 Nov 2026):** first-time customers talked as if they were known: the owner used a name they were never told, or a customer sat down and asked for "the usual" or called themselves a regular. Cause: the prompts never said who knows whom. Interim fix: `describe_relationship()` in `scripts/prompts.gd` tells the order and conversation prompts that it's a first visit, the owner doesn't know their name unless they say it, and nobody asks for "the usual". The full fix is in the Regulars and relationships stage, which replaces that function's text with what the game has actually tracked.

## Fixed

- **Food served here that isn't on the menu (fixed 15 Nov 2026):** "My friend ordered the bibimbap, said it's a game-changer", in a customer chat. Lines may still talk about other dishes (a mum's kimchi jjigae, lunch at work), but one that orders, serves or says "the" or "your" of a dish that isn't on the menu is rejected. (Mandu and hotteok are left off the list: they're favourite names for cats.)
- **Wrong pronouns in the diary and recap (fixed 15 Nov 2026):** a recap asked "Will he come back?" of a woman regular, and a diary said another "He's been MIA". The facts never said anyone's gender, so the model guessed from names it can't read, and the recap prompt's own example teaser was "Will he come back?". The facts now give each person's pronoun beside their name and in a closing "Use these pronouns" line, and the example is neutral. A plain case of the lesson above: give the model the fact.
- **"One more soju" served as somaek (fixed 15 Nov 2026):** a somaek order may mention soju and beer (it's soju in beer), so the model ordered somaek with lines like "one more soju" and "one more bottle" (somaek isn't a bottle), and they passed. A somaek order now has to ask for it; "somaeck" and "somac" are taken as somaek.
- **Rice left out of the order (fixed 15 Nov 2026):** the commonest reason an order fell back to the game's soju and odeng (9 of 11 in six nights): the line asked for rice and the items didn't have it, three times running. Rather than ask again, the game adds a side the line asks for, if the order still keeps the rules: the line wins. Drinks and mains still have to match.
- **Every visit "fine" (prompt fixed 15 Nov 2026, to check):** asked how a visit went (loved it / enjoyed it / it was fine / didn't enjoy it), Llama 3.1 8B picked "it was fine" for about four visits in five and never "loved it": the middle option. The prompt now says what each means, with "enjoyed it" as an ordinary good night. If it's still lopsided, retune the regulars' numbers to how the AI judges rather than push it further.
- **Regulars treated as strangers in chats (fixed 15 Nov 2026):** the customer-chat prompt said it was everyone's first time at the tent, so a regular of 12 years asked the owner "You must be the one who runs this place?". Chat prompts now say where each person stands, and founding regulars know each other.
- **Copied appearances (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** listing tonight's earlier appearances "to differ from" made Llama 3.1 8B copy them, nearly word for word, sometimes with the other customer's name. Removed now the game decides the look; and asking for "a detail of your own" produced the same props (crumpled notes, books, cigarettes), so the prompt asks how someone seems instead. A list of things to avoid is a list of things to copy.
- **Names nobody said (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** the game's own instructions named people ("they say goodbye to Jang Ji-Hyun"), so the AI used names that were never said. People are now named only if their name was said in the chat, and otherwise described as they look.
- **Regulars in customer chats (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** chats didn't get the "first visit" context the owner's conversations get, so customers claimed years of visits and called the ajumma "he". Chats are now told everyone's seated, new here, and who the owner is.
- **Names in Hangul (rule added 14 Nov 2026):** the AI writes each name in Hangul too; the game checks the family name and each syllable's first consonant against the romanised name, and leaves a Hangul that doesn't fit off rather than show it wrong.
- **Appearance that couldn't be drawn (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** the AI wrote each person's appearance freely, so nothing could draw it or keep it the same. The game now decides the look (age range, hair, clothes, colours, extras) and tells the AI, which invents someone who'd look like that; its appearance sentence keeps to the look and adds one detail. An age outside the game's range is moved to the nearest one inside it instead of rejected, since a whole retry for one number isn't worth it.
- **Every tent orange (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** asked to choose a new bar's tarp colour from the full list, Llama 3.1 8B picked orange nearly every time (most real pojangmacha tents are orange, the colour came first in the list, and the style guide in every prompt called it "an orange-tarp street tent bar"). The style guide now just says "a street tent bar", and the game now offers each bar a random shortlist (four colours of ten and three shapes of five) and rejects anything not offered, so the AI still chooses what suits the owner, from a set that varies. Same lesson as before: the game decides what the AI may choose from.
- **The owner nagging about food (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** "eat first", "eat the kimchi", "eat slowly", for ten lines at a time: the prompt put the table front and centre, and a small model held on to it. Fixed by having the game pick a **topic** for each spell of chat (food is one, likelier just after serving), which gave far more varied talk: stories from the owner's past, advice, the weather, hints at the secret, and the cat. Again: give the model something to do, not more rules.
- **Made-up Korean, again (rule added 15 Nov 2026):** a whole line of invented romanised Korean ("Eotteoke haeyo, yukhae? Chamcha-eun naneun joa?") still got past the prompt now and then. A line with three or more romanised-Korean-looking words (with "eo", "eu" or "ae" in them, or ending in "yo") is now rejected; known K-drama words, honorifics, names, food and places, and English look-alikes ("people", "someone", "theory") don't count.
- **Made-up Korean (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** customers spoke whole lines of invented romanised Korean with "(translations)", and it spread through a conversation once it started. Fixed by asking for English with the odd Korean word Western K-drama fans would know, and rejecting lines with brackets. None in the transcripts since.
- **Knowing what nobody said (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** the owner used a customer's reason for coming ("heard you were at a hoesik dinner"), their job ("a geologist like you") and customers knew the owner's secret ("some ex-gangster"), because prompts give the AI everyone's profile. Fixed by labelling what's private (nobody knows another's name, age, job, reason or secret unless it's been said) and adding what anyone can see (how they look).
- **Broken English (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** an owner sometimes talked in caricatured broken English ("Odeng all gone, teacher. Now, you eat, yes?"). The style rules now say they're speaking Korean, shown as fluent, natural English in their own voice.
- **Everyone alike (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** left to choose, the AI picked women about three times in four, four nurses in a night, and "dark circles under her eyes" again and again. The game now decides gender, and each new customer's details prompt lists tonight's jobs and recent looks to differ from. Every bar's cat named Ssal: the naming prompt now lists other cats' names, which are rejected.
- **Out-of-date lines (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** with the owner carrying food while the AI writes, a line could say "it's not ready yet" just after it arrived. Now a line is asked for again if that customer's table or the cat changed while it was being written.
- **Same names, slow arrivals (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** with several people at the counter, new customers were often rejected for sharing a name, and arrivals got slow: Llama 3.1 8B reaches for the same handful of names (Kim Ji-ho, Lee Min-jun...), and each rejection cost a whole customer reply. Fixed by inventing customers in **two steps**: first just a name and gender (a tiny reply, so a retry is cheap), then the rest of that person. The name prompt lists the names at the counter (which are rejected) and names used earlier tonight (to avoid), and asks for names real Koreans have, "not always the most common ones". The lesson again: change what the model is asked to produce rather than adding instructions.
- **The cat as it really is (rule added 14 Nov 2026):** with a cat in the tent, small models happily have people stroke it or talk of it purring on their lap wherever it actually is. The prompt says where the cat is right now, and the game rejects any line where the cat's stroked or on a lap unless it's on the lap of someone in that conversation. How often this trips will show in the stats ("the cat").
- **Strangers using close terms (rule added 14 Nov 2026; replaced 15 Nov 2026 by the address rules, which decide who may call whom what):** with customers chatting, small models like to throw in oppa, hyung, nuna and eonni, which Koreans only use with people they're close to. Every line is now checked for them (in their common spellings), with names like Hyung-jun excluded, and the chat prompt says the speakers are strangers who talk politely. Rare in practice so far (about one rejection a night).
- **"Spicy rice cakes" read as rice (fixed 14 Nov 2026):** customers order tteokbokki by its description, and "rice" in "rice cakes" matched Gonggibap's alias, so the order was rejected as mentioning rice. "Rice cakes" is now an alias for tteokbokki (longest phrase first, so it's not also read as rice).
- **Later orders that ask for more than they get (fixed 13 Nov 2026):** a customer's later order sometimes asked for things that never arrived (e.g. "one more makgeolli and another tteokbokki" with only makgeolli ordered). The serving was fine; the line check had two holes: for later orders it let anything already on the table through, and it only looked for the first word of each menu name, so aliases like "rice" went unnoticed. Fixed in `scripts/rules/orders.gd`: everything a later order's line asks for must be in `"items"`, and a dish already on the table can only be mentioned as "this/the/my … " (`REFERRING_WORDS`); order lines now also catch aliases, longest phrase first, so "fried rice" isn't also read as "rice" (`mentions()`). Conversation lines still ignore aliases, so "have you eaten rice?" stays a greeting. The later-order prompt now explains both rules.
- **Talking about food that isn't on the table (fixed 13 Nov 2026):** a customer ordered makgeolli and two ramyeon, then both she and the owner talked about tteokbokki ("I put in extra tteokbokki, like I said"). Cause: our own style guide said "steam rising off the tteokbokki" in every prompt, the most vivid food the AI saw. Fixed by removing every menu item from the style guide ("green bottles on every table, steam rising off the pots"), and by checking every conversation line against what's on the table: a line mentioning another menu item is rejected and retried (`unordered_mention()` in `scripts/rules/orders.gd`).
- **Unrealistic orders (fixed 13 Nov 2026):** customers often ordered two ramyeon and tteokbokki with no drink; after a prompt tweak said "a drink or two", nearly everyone ordered two drinks. Fixed by making ordering habits game rules (`check_order_shape()` in `scripts/rules/orders.gd`): every order has at least one drink and one food; foods are marked **main** or **side** on the menu; at most one main and one drink, unless the game has made the customer **hungry** (15%: two mains) or given them a **rough night** (20%: two drinks). The order prompt explains the habits and says which kind of customer this is. Free **banchan** (kimchi and pickled radish) now comes with every order, off the bill, as at a real pojangmacha. Lesson: a casual phrase like "a drink or two" in a prompt is read as an instruction.
- **Everyone orders exactly once (fixed 13 Nov 2026):** real K-drama customers order more partway through: one more bottle, a side, rice to finish the sauce. Added **later orders**: the game decides how many (half never, 40% once more, 10% a third time; `EXTRA_ORDER_ODDS`) and when. A later order is one or two items, at most one drink, no new mains, and its line must sound like adding more ("one more bottle", "rice too"), not like a first order (`MORE_WORDS`). Added **gonggibap** (a bowl of rice) and **bokkeumbap** (fried rice in the leftover sauce, only as a later order and only after a main) to the menu. Later orders first came too early, often straight after the food arrived, because they could land on the customer's first reply and short conversations left no room; now they never do, each later order adds an exchange to the conversation, and two later orders always have an ordinary line between them (`extra_order_turns()`).

- **Double opening and mismatched orders (fixed 12 Nov 2026):** customers said an opening line, then ordered again, and what they said didn't match what was served (e.g. "one soju, please", then "one of everything", then served tteokbokki, beer and gyeran-mari). Cause: the opening line was written when the customer was invented, before the AI had seen the menu. A prompt tweak didn't fix it. Fixed by restructuring: in a visit, the customer's first words come from the order step, as one line (greeting plus order), and the game rejects a line that mentions any menu item not in the order.
- **Owner titles (fixed 12 Nov 2026):** the AI gave owners titles that didn't fit: a man's title on a woman ("Kim Sun-young (Ajeossi)", a "gruff-but-soft ajumma"), then relationship terms like *oppa* and *hyungnim*, which only work when a particular kind of person is speaking. Fixed in two steps. The game picks the owner's gender and sets their title itself: a woman is always **Ajumma** and a man always **Ajusshi** (`OWNER_ADDRESS` in `scripts/prompts.gd`). Customers may use that or a safe alternative (Imo, Sajangnim, Eomeoni / Samchon, Sajangnim), but never oppa, hyung, nuna or eonni. The game also rejects an owner whose archetype uses a term for the other gender (`GENDERED_TERMS` in `scripts/visit.gd`). Owners are now 40 or older, to suit the titles.
