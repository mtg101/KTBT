# KTBT: LLM Tuning notes

Quirks we've noticed in the AI's output, why they happen, and how we handle them. Open issues are waiting for a fix; fixed ones stay here as a record of what works with a small local model, and each should get a test (see [TESTS.md](TESTS.md#bugs-become-tests)).

**Lesson so far:** when a small model keeps doing something odd, changing what it's asked to produce, or having the game decide and check the fact itself, works better than adding more instructions to the prompt.

## Open

- **Not listening to each other (partly fixed 13 Nov 2026, needs more work):** the owner and customer reply as if they hadn't heard each other. Example: Kim Ji-hyun orders "a cold beer and a plate of tteokbokki", both are served, and the owner replies "One beer, okay. But you eat, no?", then she answers "I'm good, thanks". Likely causes: (1) the transcript the AI sees holds only dialogue, so the serving never appears in it, and the owner's first line reads like a reply to the order rather than coming after the food arrives; (2) the line to respond to is buried in the middle of a long prompt, and small models pay most attention to the end; (3) the style guide's strong tropes ("scolds customers for not eating properly") pull harder than the facts. Done so far: the serving is now recorded in the transcript as a stage direction ("(Kim Min-seok serves: Beer, Tteokbokki.)"), and every dialogue prompt ends with a **WHAT JUST HAPPENED** recap of the last line said and anything after it, telling the AI to reply directly and stay consistent (`what_just_happened()` in `scripts/prompts.gd`). Conversations are noticeably better, but still not great. Still to try: toning down how hard the style guide pushes tropes, and comparing models (Llama 3.1 8B, Gemma, Qwen) with AI quality runs.
- **Name order:** the AI sometimes writes names Western-style (e.g. "Ji-hoon Kim" instead of "Kim Ji-hoon"), despite being asked for family name first. Minor. Fix later with a prompt example, or by having the AI give family and given names as separate fields that the game joins in the right order. Related idea: non-Korean visitors, in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).
- **"You've been here before" (open, parked for the Regulars and relationships stage):** the owner sometimes treats a first-time customer as a regular ("you've been here before, haven't you?"), ignoring the first-visit note. Same root cause as the entry below: a trope (the owner who knows everyone) beating a fact. To be fixed properly when the game tracks who knows whom.
- **Strangers acting like regulars (interim fix 12 Nov 2026):** first-time customers talked as if they were known: the owner used a name they were never told, or a customer sat down and asked for "the usual" or called themselves a regular. Cause: the prompts never said who knows whom. Interim fix: `describe_relationship()` in `scripts/prompts.gd` tells the order and conversation prompts that it's a first visit, the owner doesn't know their name unless they say it, and nobody asks for "the usual". The full fix is in the Regulars and relationships stage, which replaces that function's text with what the game has actually tracked.

## Fixed

- **Later orders that ask for more than they get (fixed 13 Nov 2026):** a customer's later order sometimes asked for things that never arrived (e.g. "one more makgeolli and another tteokbokki" with only makgeolli ordered). The serving was fine; the line check had two holes: for later orders it let anything already on the table through, and it only looked for the first word of each menu name, so aliases like "rice" went unnoticed. Fixed in `scripts/rules.gd`: everything a later order's line asks for must be in `"items"`, and a dish already on the table can only be mentioned as "this/the/my … " (`REFERRING_WORDS`); order lines now also catch aliases, longest phrase first, so "fried rice" isn't also read as "rice" (`mentions()`). Conversation lines still ignore aliases, so "have you eaten rice?" stays a greeting. The later-order prompt now explains both rules.
- **Talking about food that isn't on the table (fixed 13 Nov 2026):** a customer ordered makgeolli and two ramyeon, then both she and the owner talked about tteokbokki ("I put in extra tteokbokki, like I said"). Cause: our own style guide said "steam rising off the tteokbokki" in every prompt, the most vivid food the AI saw. Fixed by removing every menu item from the style guide ("green bottles on every table, steam rising off the pots"), and by checking every conversation line against what's on the table: a line mentioning another menu item is rejected and retried (`unordered_mention()` in `scripts/rules.gd`).
- **Unrealistic orders (fixed 13 Nov 2026):** customers often ordered two ramyeon and tteokbokki with no drink; after a prompt tweak said "a drink or two", nearly everyone ordered two drinks. Fixed by making ordering habits game rules (`check_order_shape()` in `scripts/rules.gd`): every order has at least one drink and one food; foods are marked **main** or **side** on the menu; at most one main and one drink, unless the game has made the customer **hungry** (15%: two mains) or given them a **rough night** (20%: two drinks). The order prompt explains the habits and says which kind of customer this is. Free **banchan** (kimchi and pickled radish) now comes with every order, off the bill, as at a real pojangmacha. Lesson: a casual phrase like "a drink or two" in a prompt is read as an instruction.
- **Everyone orders exactly once (fixed 13 Nov 2026):** real K-drama customers order more partway through: one more bottle, a side, rice to finish the sauce. Added **later orders**: the game decides how many (half never, 40% once more, 10% a third time; `EXTRA_ORDER_ODDS`) and when. A later order is one or two items, at most one drink, no new mains, and its line must sound like adding more ("one more bottle", "rice too"), not like a first order (`MORE_WORDS`). Added **gonggibap** (a bowl of rice) and **bokkeumbap** (fried rice in the leftover sauce, only as a later order and only after a main) to the menu. Later orders first came too early, often straight after the food arrived, because they could land on the customer's first reply and short conversations left no room; now they never do, each later order adds an exchange to the conversation, and two later orders always have an ordinary line between them (`extra_order_turns()`).

- **Double opening and mismatched orders (fixed 12 Nov 2026):** customers said an opening line, then ordered again, and what they said didn't match what was served (e.g. "one soju, please", then "one of everything", then served tteokbokki, beer and gyeran-mari). Cause: the opening line was written when the customer was invented, before the AI had seen the menu. A prompt tweak didn't fix it. Fixed by restructuring: in a visit, the customer's first words come from the order step, as one line (greeting plus order), and the game rejects a line that mentions any menu item not in the order.
- **Owner titles (fixed 12 Nov 2026):** the AI gave owners titles that didn't fit: a man's title on a woman ("Kim Sun-young (Ajeossi)", a "gruff-but-soft ajumma"), then relationship terms like *oppa* and *hyungnim*, which only work when a particular kind of person is speaking. Fixed in two steps. The game picks the owner's gender and sets their title itself: a woman is always **Ajumma** and a man always **Ajusshi** (`OWNER_ADDRESS` in `scripts/prompts.gd`). Customers may use that or a safe alternative (Imo, Sajangnim, Eomeoni / Samchon, Sajangnim), but never oppa, hyung, nuna or eonni. The game also rejects an owner whose archetype uses a term for the other gender (`GENDERED_TERMS` in `scripts/visit.gd`). Owners are now 40 or older, to suit the titles.
